## Design Context

### Users
The owner rides an NMAX near Benoa / Nusa Dua and wants to choose a petrol station using congestion lengths, accepting either Pertalite or Pertamax.

### Brand Personality
Clear, calm, useful. Follow the creator's existing Bali Cinema Map and Australia 462 map references.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light basemap, compact left panel, restrained green and amber, searchable station list, and visible selection. Use one continuous page scroll on phones, as established in the cinema map. Put secondary explanation and settings behind a single help control.

### Design Principles
- Station selection and the map are the first screen.
- Show measured congestion and when it was observed; never imply archived observations are a live feed or confirmed fuel stock.
- Keep traffic, directions, location selection and comparison within easy reach.
- Use real station coordinates, accessible keyboard controls and generous touch targets.
- Keep private home details out of published assets.
